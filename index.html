Shift Audit & Control

Every Shift Reconciled to the Last Pound.

From float handover... to cash drawer reconciliation.

At shift closing, ZAAD aggregates cashier sales, calculates expected drawer cash, compares it with actual cash entered, and highlights matching status, shortage, or surplus.

After closing, the shift remains archived with date, responsible cashier, duration, and financials for audit whenever needed.

Inventory Count & Stock Audit

Count your inventory... without starting from scratch every time.

Start a count session and scan barcodes or search by name. ZAAD instantly compares physical quantities against system stock, revealing matching items, shortages, and surpluses before committing stock adjustments.

Paused your count? Pick up right where you left off.

Draft stock counts are saved locally and can be resumed even after restarting the application.

Performing a partial count? Uncounted stock remains untouched.

When committing the audit, only counted items are updated—all uncounted catalog items stay completely untouched.
