Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026 • Official Domain: zaadsystem.com • Contact: support@zaadsystem.com

1. Overview & Data Ownership

This Privacy Policy governs the operation of ZAAD POS (zaadsystem.com), developed and maintained by Gehad Qabel. All store transactions, inventory, customer accounts, supplier balances, shift records, and financial summaries remain 100% stored on the local desktop computer hardware.

2. Local Storage Architecture

All store data is stored locally in an embedded SQLite database on the store's hard drive. No cloud syncing or external database hosting is required.

3. Google API & OAuth 2.0 Integration Disclosures

ZAAD POS offers an optional feature enabling store owners to receive automated Daily Z reports sent directly to their email via Gmail using scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send.

The Gmail permission is used strictly to compose and dispatch outgoing Daily Z reports. ZAAD POS does NOT read, view, parse, or scan inbox messages. Google authentication tokens are stored locally on the desktop system.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking

ZAAD POS does not include third-party tracking scripts, advertising trackers, analytics pixels, or behavioral cookies.
